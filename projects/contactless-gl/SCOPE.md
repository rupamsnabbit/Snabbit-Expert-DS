## Design problems

Named at intake because an open question is not the right shape for them — they are what the design has to solve.

**1. The permission the phone asks for, in a language she cannot read.** The OS dialog cannot be translated or restyled. She may not know what it is asking, and a mis-tap (*Deny*, *Don't ask again*, the wrong toggle on a settings page) is one tap away. What the design controls: everything before the dialog (the spoken `permission` sheet, whose **Allow …** label and picture prepare her for the exact button she will see), everything after it (the app detects what she chose and shows the way back, never a second dialog), and the settings-page trips (a clip for her phone maker, then *Open settings*, then a check on return). The denied and permanently-denied paths are designed as first-class screens, not edge cases, and the per-maker clip shows the real dialog with the right button circled. Research input: Mobbin permission flows from worker-side apps, and the five makers' actual dialogs. Owner: this project; lands in `patterns/permissions.md`.
