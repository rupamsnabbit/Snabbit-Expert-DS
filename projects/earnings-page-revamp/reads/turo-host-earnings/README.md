## What the screens show

*Business* › tabs *EARNINGS · PERFORMANCE · REVIEWS* · *All vehicles* · **$19 earned in 2025** · *All earnings adjustments included* · a year bar chart (Jan → Jul) with a tooltip *MAR 2025 · $19* on the tapped bar · **a legend of five coloured buckets, each with its amount**: *$19 Trip earnings ⓘ · $0 Upcoming earnings ⓘ · $0 Reimbursements ⓘ · $0 Incentives ⓘ · $0 Missed earnings ⓘ* · rows *Transaction history › · Tax information ›* · *EARNING TIPS* rows · filters *All vehicles ▾ · 2025 ▾*.

## 1 · The idea

Earnings are **a small fixed set of named buckets, each with its own colour and amount, and the total is their sum** — the same five names every month.

## 2 · Idea vs. expression

| Borrowable | Skin |
| --- | --- |
| **Five buckets, always the same, each with amount** — the user learns the set once | five colours as the only identity (fails greyscale) |
| *Missed earnings* as a bucket — money she could have had is shown, not hidden | `$0` rows kept visible |
| *All earnings adjustments included* — one line that says the number is final | the ⓘ on every bucket |
| Tap a bar → the month's figure | the chart itself (hosts have years; she has a month) |

## 3 · Against the expert

| Failure | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Colour is the bucket's only identity | one **icon** per bucket (`briefcase` · `arrow-circle-up` · `arrow-circle-down`), colour never alone |
| Five buckets, two of them abstractions (*Upcoming*, *Reimbursements*) | three — *Earned · Added · Taken* — because that is the arithmetic she does |
| `$0` rows | hidden (`statement.md`) |
| A year is the frame | a month is the frame; the calendar replaces the chart |

## 4 · Tier

Nothing new; the Statement's rows are the legend.

## 5–6 · What travels into the pattern

- **The fixed bucket set** — the same three rows on every Statement, in the same order, with the same icons. Turo's five became three because three is what *Earned + Added − Taken = Payout* needs.
- **Say the number is final** — a one-line caption on the month hero when the month is closed: *Final · nothing more is added*; while open: *Till today*.
- **Dropped**: colour identity, the chart, `$0` rows, ⓘ.
