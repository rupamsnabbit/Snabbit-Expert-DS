# Reference read — why should she come back? (Home top section, 2026-10-21)

Internal only. Never published, never traced.

**Feedback the lead got:** the Home top card (*₹2,600 waiting for you · Login to claim now*) is not inviting, and the
winback bonus on its own is not a convincing reason to come back.

## 1 · Diagnosis (before looking)

- **It's her own money.** Showing "₹2,600 waiting for you" is a refund of something that was taken from her. It reads as
  getting back what's hers, not as a gain, so it doesn't pull.
- **No path.** "Login to claim" doesn't say what she must do, or how soon the money arrives.
- **One reason only.** Money is one of four reasons an expert returns. The others are work near her, the status she
  built (tier, rating), and the money. The card shows only one.

## 2 · What was looked at (Mobbin, iOS)

| App | Screen | Pattern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Natural AI | Reactivate subscription | One hero number (total saved), then three icon rows of *what you get*, then one button |
| Opal | Personalised promise | Her own numbers lead ("9 years back"); three icon rows, each with a number |
| Walmart+ | Reactivate | "Ready to start saving again?" — a warm question, the plan, one button |
| Runna | Welcome back | "You've been inactive a while" — then concrete ways to restart, no guilt |
| DoorDash Dasher | Home | "Kick off the day with lots of offers": demand near her is the reason to go online, money comes second |
| Duolingo · ShopBack · TextNow | Quests | A reward broken into steps; each step is a progress bar and its prize sits beside it |
| Crypto.com | Rewards stations | Locked stages; what the next stage unlocks is visible, and so is the step to get there |

## 3 · Structure that recurs

The reasons are **added up** into one number, then itemised (Natural AI, Opal). The way to get the money is a short
**path** with its own progress, not a claim button (Duolingo, ShopBack). For workers, **work available** is the lead
reason, not the bonus (DoorDash). Restarting is framed as easy and guilt-free (Runna, Walmart).

## 4 · Translation and tier

Four directions, each a Home → sheet → after flow on the lead's inactive Home (`545:63660`). Composition (Tier 2):
the card slot and the attendance card stay the same; the rows are the ledger's row anatomy (ListItem, 40 disc, 16/14).
Not taken: countdown clocks, "limited time", strike-through, confetti on an inactive screen (P6).
