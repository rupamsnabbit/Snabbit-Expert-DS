## Boundary

This project reads **its own folder** and **the system** (`tokens/`, `components/`, `foundations/`, `principles/`, `patterns/`, `references/SCREENS.md`). It does not read any other project's folder. Every decision traces to this PRD, the principles, or the tokens — nothing else.

**What design owns here.** The PRD is mostly engine: configs, ledger, invariants, edge cases (§ 3–6, 9–10). Design answers § 7 (*Expert app UI*) and the words and spoken lines of § 8 (*comms*). The engine sections are read as the source of states and numbers, not designed.
