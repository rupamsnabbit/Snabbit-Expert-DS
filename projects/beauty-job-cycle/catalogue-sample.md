## The job used across the Paper board

One job, drawn consistently on every screen that names it, so the board reads as one continuous story.

> **Radhika S** — Tuesday 7 February, 10:00 am – 11:15 am
> **Fruit facial** (60 min) · **Eyebrow threading** (15 min)
> 9 products · **You will earn ₹145 + ₹5**
> HAL Old Airport Rd, near railway over bridge

*Radhika S* and the address are the **shipped** strings (`4:8915` and others) — kept so the Beauty board and the
Househelp board show the same customer and read as one library. Earnings are the shipped figures too; § 1 says
Beauty earnings are recomputed from services booked, but the real pricing is not in the PRD, so the shipped number
stands in and is flagged.

## The § 6 case

*Products you used* shows all 9, grouped by service. The **difference** path uses one realistic miss:

> Cotton pads — **+2 pcs vs usual 4** → reason: *client needed more*

One changed line out of nine is the honest case: the PRD's instruction is *"only the products that differed need to
change — everything else stays at usual."* Drawing three or four changes would misrepresent the effort.
