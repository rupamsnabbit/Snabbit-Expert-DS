# Pinterest · voice assistant UIs (2026-10-14)

These pins are for mood only (EXTENDING.md: nothing structural is borrowed from unshipped screens). The lead supplied Pinterest after the first Feedback 2 attempt was rejected.

## What the pins share
- A full-bleed soft aurora gradient (pink, lilac, peach) instead of grey and white app chrome.
- One glowing entity (orb or blob) as the only hero.
- Large captions in karaoke style: spoken words dark, upcoming words faint.
- A bottom row of round controls: a central mic flanked by small glass circles (close, keyboard).
- Glass surfaces: translucent white with a white hairline.

## Translation (Feedback 2 · v2)
- Saathi lives inside a glossy orb on a Snabbit-pink aurora. She is the hero, not a disabled button.
- The cards become two pearl tiles, Place and Time, using the same pearl gradients and linework. A tile lights up while Saathi mentions it and fills with the expert's words, with a green tick.
- While Saathi speaks there is **no control at all**, only a quiet "Saathi is speaking" status. The mic arrives in the bottom row (Replay · Mic · Type) when it is her turn.
- Listening: the same button becomes Stop inside a live waveform, and her words appear as the caption.
- Got it: Saathi gets a green tick, the answer is read back, and the actions are *Find work* and *Say again*.
