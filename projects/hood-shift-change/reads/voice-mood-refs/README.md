## Read

1. **What it does.** 01: the whole screen points at one action, and the arrow is the instruction. 02: while you speak, the waveform is the screen, and the words sit underneath.
2. **Why it works.** One focal point each. Motion and shape say "speak here" and "I hear you" without a sentence.
3. **Borrowed (structure and interaction):** the glow as the stage; an arrow drawn into the button; the waveform as the hero during recording; the words below it, the latest darkest.
4. **Tier:** an off-system exploration at the lead's request (row J on *Voice flow · fresh*). If chosen: the glow stage, the sticker chip and the drawn arrow become asks of core (a token for the glow, a VoiceInput variant, an asset for the arrow). Nothing ships as a fork.
5. **Not borrowed:** the serif and condensed display faces (Outfit stays), black as the action colour (pink is the action colour she knows; P6), English-only copy.
6. **Translation:** J1–J3 beside I1–I3; the differences are listed in the row's label and in `SCOPE.md` → Log.
