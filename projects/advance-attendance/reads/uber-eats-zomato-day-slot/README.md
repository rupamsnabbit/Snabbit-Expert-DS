1. **Idea.** Day tabs across the top (*Today · Tomorrow · Wed*), slot rows below; one day visible at a time; the primary Button commits.
2. **Idea vs skin.** Borrowable: *one day at a time* with the other days one tap away; slots as full-width rows with a control. Skin: radio lists of 12 slots, wheels (Zomato), prices.
3. **Against the expert.** Three apps she may already know use this shape — it is convention (P7, keep what she knows). Fails when the slot list is long; ours is two to three rows, so it does not.
4. **Tier.** Tabs `segmented` + Selection controls rows, or two screens (a step is a screen). Nothing new.
