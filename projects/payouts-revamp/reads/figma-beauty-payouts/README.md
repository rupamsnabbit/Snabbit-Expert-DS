## 1 · Month statement (`814:15715`)

Same frame as the Househelp month, with three Beauty differences:

- **A headline above the Statement: *Total Business ₹1,00,000*** (Heading, no card) — the value of the services she sold. Then the Statement band *Monthly Earnings ₹50,000*.
- Deductions are **Bike EMI · Deducted EMI (3/11)** · **Bike rental** · **Onboarding kit and Fees · Deducted EMI (1/6)** → *Remaining Payout ₹5,000* → schedule *15 Feb Monthly payout*.
- *Monthly Earnings Breakup* rows: *Daily Earnings ₹28,000 › · **Product Cost −₹8,000 ›** · Joining Bonus ⓘ 🔒 ₹1,500 · Winback Bonus ⓘ 🔒 ₹5,000 · Adjustments ₹8,000 · Income Tax (20 %) −₹4,200 · Upload your PAN Card to reduce tax · **Tips · Check ›*** → *Total Earnings ₹11,800 + 🔒 ₹8,500*. **No Karma bonus row and no Gold coins tile**: *Penalties* holds only *Red Cards · received this month · 5*. *Other Actions*: *Request Early Payout · Available limit ₹25,000 · View Payout History · Referral Earnings*, then the two Add UPI / Add PAN callouts.

## 3 · Day (`814:15475` … `1153:49419`)

Top to bottom:

1. Day pager *‹ 13 March 2025 ›*.
2. **Tier band** across the screen — *No Tier* (brown) · *Silver* · *Gold* · *Diamond* · *Pink Diamond*, each in the tier's tint with its badge.
3. **Segmented control *All shifts · First shift · Second shift***. *All shifts* shows *Shift info* as a horizontal carousel of shift cards with a page indicator; a single shift shows one card. Cards: green header *Present · First shift / Second shift*, *Shift Timing (5h) 7:00 AM – 12:00 PM* / *3:00 PM – 7:00 PM*, login and logout times.
4. A floating line ✓ *Minimum Business Guarantee (MinBG) = ₹2000*.
5. **Statement** *Daily Earnings ₹530*:
   - *Business Pay ⓘ ₹1,800* → inset *Services · Distance Bonus*
   - **Differential MinBG Pay ⓘ ₹200** → inset *MinBG ₹2,000 · Business Pay −₹1,800 · Inventory Adjustment (25 %) −₹50* (*25% of ₹200* / *25% of (MinBG − Business Pay)*). On the second-shift frame *Inventory Adjustment (25 %) −₹50* is its own top-level row instead.
   - **Daily Pass −₹100**
   - *Red Cards 🟥0 ₹0 ›*
6. *Earnings Breakup*: one card per job — *Job ID #12563 | 6:05 PM ₹160* · *Business Pay: Services (1.5 hr) ₹1,230* → inset *Waxing (1 hr) ₹950 · Massage (30 min) ₹200 · Long distance ₹5*.

What the Beauty model is, from the screens: she is paid on **business** (the services she does), with a **daily minimum business guarantee** (MinBG ₹2,000). When business falls short of MinBG she gets the gap as *Differential MinBG Pay*, **less 25 % of the gap as an inventory adjustment** (product she did not use). She **pays a Daily Pass** each day, cheaper at higher tiers. **Product cost** is deducted at month level. The day view also shows *Business Pay* rows that are not all money to her — the job card's *₹1,230 Business Pay* sits under a *₹160* job total — **to confirm** how business turns into her pay (commission? share?).
