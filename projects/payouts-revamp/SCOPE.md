## Boundary

This project reads **its own folder** and **the system** (`tokens/`, `components/`, `foundations/`, `principles/`, `patterns/`, `references/SCREENS.md`). It does not read any other project's folder. Every decision traces to this PRD, the principles, or the tokens — nothing else.

Two other projects touch the same surface and were **not opened**: `earnings-page-revamp` (status *Building*, "the Earnings first screen, the end-of-day sheet, the month statement") and `emi-recovery` ("a section in `patterns/payout.md`"). Their reference folders under `references/earnings-page-revamp-*` and `references/earnings-revamp-figma-2026-10-05/` were not opened either. See *Open questions*.
