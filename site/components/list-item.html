Core component

ListItem

One row in a list: a leading thing, a title with optional lines, a trailing thing, a divider. Eleven Figma frames are all this row with different things in the slots. Tall enough to tap, calm enough to scan, honest about status.

ProposedTier 1 · CoreSnabbitListItem56 · 64 · 72 dpReplaces 11 frames
Sizing

Heights & lines

Title only 56; with a description 64; with two lines or a 40 dp leading 72. Title is BodyLg 16 — the row is what she reads, so it gets the reading size. Three lines maximum; more is a Card.

Composition

Slots

Leading: icon, tonal icon box, avatar, badge, checkbox/radio, pictogram, or none. Trailing: Amount, Tag, caret, Amount + caret, Switch, meta, or one IconButton. Never two trailing elements except Amount + caret; never a Button in a row.

Behaviour

States

Status is a line with an icon and words in the status colour — never a red label alone. Loading is three skeleton rows. No swipe actions: a gesture must never be the only path.

In context

Live lists

A full-bleed jobs list with section headers, and the earnings breakdown from Figma rebuilt — including the hidden value, which is now ₹•••• with an eye, not a blur. Tap the eye. Toggle the switch row.
