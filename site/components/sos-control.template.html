SOSControl

One control, one shape, one place, that calls for help when she cannot read, cannot look, and may not be able to speak. Impossible to miss, impossible to trigger by accident, impossible to lose once triggered.

Shape

Control & gesture

The resting form is the HeaderAction she already knows — a 30 dp disc with the siren and an SOS label pill — always the last thing on the right of the Home and in-job headers. A hold of 1.5 seconds triggers it. Because her thumb covers the control, the progress ring appears in the centre of the screen. Releasing early cancels with a spoken Toast.

Try it

Live

Press and hold SOS in the header (mouse or touch). Ticks at 0.5 and 1.0 s, trigger at 1.5 s. On the active screen the 5-second cancel is a plain tap; I'm safe now is a hold — an accidental end is worse than an accidental start.

Outcome

Active screen

Full-screen takeover, tab bar and header hidden, back does nothing, screen stays on. First five seconds: countdown and a plain-tap cancel. Then the calmest copy in the app — Help is on the way. Stay on the line. — and a live row for each thing that is happening, because nothing about safety is hidden or implied.

Persistent

Safety strip

A 32 dp strip under the header whenever anything safety-related is live: SOS in the background, Shield recording, location shared. It never hides, never scrolls, cannot be dismissed; it ends when the state ends. Tapping opens the relevant screen.

Patchy data is normal

Offline

  1. No data, has voice: the call goes through the dialler to the safety line; location by SMS when possible. Rows say Calling by phone · Location by SMS.
  2. No signal: the active screen still opens; rows say No network — trying…; retry every 10 s; Call police 112 still attempts the emergency call.
  3. Silent phone: haptics still fire; the hold ring and the screen carry the meaning.
  4. The control is never disabled, greyed or hidden — not offline, not logged out mid-job, not during a job alert.
