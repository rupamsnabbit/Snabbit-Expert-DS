Core component

PageIndicator

The small row of dots under anything she swipes sideways: you are on the second of four. It exists only where there is a carousel, a carousel exists only for things she can safely miss, and there are never more than five pages.

Anatomy

Current page a 24 × 8 pill in bgBrand, the rest 8 dp dots in borderDefault, 8 dp gaps, centred 12 dp below the carousel on the page background — never on the image. An inverse variant for full-screen intros on a coloured stage.

Try it

Live

Swipe or drag the cards (or use the buttons). The pill slides with the page. A promotional carousel may auto-advance every 6 s, pausing while touched and stopping after one loop; the how-to clips never do.

Limits

Rules

  1. 2–5 pages. One page has no indicator; six is a vertical list or three cards plus See all ›.
  2. Not a target. The swipe (or the flow's Next Button) moves pages; an 8 dp dot on a moving bus is a mis-tap.
  3. Auto-advance only for promotional banners: 6 s, pauses on touch, one loop, never restarts. Never for how-to clips or anything with a Button inside. Never with a screen reader or reduced motion.
  4. Below the card, on the page background — never over the image.
  5. Announces "Page 2 of 4" on change; each card is a region with its own title.

What changes in Figma

  1. Create PageIndicator: Count (2–5), Current (1–5), Variant (Default / Inverse). Pill bgBrand; dots borderDefault.
  2. Replace the dash + dots under the rate-card clips and the Home banners with instances 12 dp below the card, on the page background.
  3. Trim any carousel mock with more than five cards.

Result: ~8 variants, and one indicator for every swipe.
