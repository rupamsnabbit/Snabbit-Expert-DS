Core component

BottomTabBar

The whole map of the app, always visible, always in the same order. For a user who struggles with hierarchical navigation this is the only structure she needs to learn. Everything is reachable from one of these tabs in at most two steps.

ProposedTier 1 · CoreSnabbitBottomTabBar4 tabs · fixed orderPill indicatorNo Figma component yet
Fixed

Destinations

Four, in this order, in every build — the bar the app already ships and the experts have already learned. Icons become registered Phosphor glyphs; the spray bottle stays because it is her tool and it is known. New areas go inside a tab; only Rewards may ever become a fifth. Saathi is never a tab — it lives, labelled, in the Home header.

Behaviour

States

Selected is three channels: a 64 × 32 bgBrandSubtle pill behind the icon, the icon's weight (regular → fill) and brand colour on icon and label. Labels are always visible; an icon-only tab bar is not allowed. Badges: dot, count ≤ 9, or a promo amount — promo on Refer only, while the bonus is live. At most two badged tabs at once; Home never badges.

Try it

Live

Switch tabs — instant, no slide; each tab keeps its scroll. Badges clear when the tab opens. Tapping the current tab scrolls it to the top.

Rules

Always visible on tab roots and one level down. Hidden only on full-screen flows that must not be left mid-way: job alert, check-in camera, OTP. Never hides on scroll.

Labels wrap to two lines at 130 % font scale and the bar grows to 72 dp — they never truncate or hide. The ₹4000 promo pill on Refer does not clear on open — it is a fact, not an unread count.

What changes in Figma

  1. Create BottomTabBar: Tabs (4 / 5), Selected (1–5), per-tab Badge (None / Dot / Count / Promo).
  2. Phosphor instances — spray-bottle, currency-inr, users, user-circle — regular unselected, fill selected. Replace the custom spray-bottle glyph.
  3. Selected pill 64 × 32 bound to bgBrandSubtle, not a raw pink.
  4. Add the bar to every tab-root and one-level-down screen; remove hamburger menus and drawer frames.

Result: ~12 variants, and a bottom edge every screen agrees on.
