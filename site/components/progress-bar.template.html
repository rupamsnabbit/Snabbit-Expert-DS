Core component

ProgressBar

How far she has come toward something she can name — the next tier, this week's coins, five jobs today. A filling track with the goal at the end. Never a loading indicator, never a percentage.

ProposedTier 1 · CoreSnabbitProgressBarlinear · segmentedvalue = "4 of 20"
Shape

Kinds & sizes

Linear for a continuous quantity, segmented (2–7) for a count of equal things. Three sizes; the label row is part of the component so the value is never left out. The value is always a number and a noun — 4 of 20 coins — never %.

Computed

Tones

Tone follows the value: brand while she is on the way, success when the goal is reached (fill turns green, a check joins the value, the asset lights), neutral for a track that is locked or not yet open. Never warning or error — a bar that turns red is a punishment; a shortfall is told by a Banner.

Slots

Markers

A goal asset at the end of the track (metallic layer — coin, tier badge), lit when reached, grey with a lock when not yet available. Milestones are dots on the track at their values with Small labels beneath — the one place Small is allowed, because the value line above carries the meaning.

Try it

Live

Fill animates 300 ms from the previous value. Reaching the goal completes the fill, then the asset pulses once with the coin sound and a medium haptic. A decrease animates down silently — the Banner that caused it does the talking.

Compositions

The Figma Progress Cards and TIER PROGRESS frames become three compositions built from Card, ListItem and ProgressBar. Only here may the fill take the colour of the next tier's asset — because the asset is right there at the end of the track.

Reconciliation

What changes in Figma

  1. Progress Bar → ProgressBar: Kind (Linear / Segmented), Size (S / M / L), Marker (None / Goal / Milestones), Tone (Brand / Success / Neutral). Fill level is a number property, not a variant. Bind track to bgTertiary, fill to bgBrand / iconSuccess.
  2. Delete the dark-grey and light-grey fill variants; delete the Progress Buttons frame — a filling button is the Button's hold-to-confirm state.
  3. Progress Cards → TierProgressCard (Card + ProgressBar M goal) and WeeklyGoalCard (ProgressBar M milestones).
  4. TIER PROGRESS → TierRail composition: ProgressBar L with five tier assets as milestones and the Avatar position marker.
  5. Progress Stat – List → ListItem with ProgressBar S trailing.

Result: ~12 variants replacing 34 + 16 + 5 + 8 frames.
