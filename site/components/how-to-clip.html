Core component

HowToClip

A ten-second film of a real expert doing the thing, with a voice saying what she is doing. The third channel — after showing and saying — and the one first-time users completed tasks with in the research. A card she taps; a player that shows one task, loops once, speaks, and gets out of the way.

In a carousel or a row

Card

4:5 thumbnail (the first frame), a play disc, a duration chip so she knows the cost before tapping, a title of ≤ 3 words. ≤ 5 in a carousel with a PageIndicator; a small one can sit in a Stepper row or a Coachmark. Watched cards carry a check.

Try it

Player · live

Full-screen portrait. Autoplays with voice, captions always on, knob-less progress. Tap the video to pause and resume. It loops once, then stops on the last frame with Replay — never Up next. Close returns to where she was.

Making and showing clips

Rules

  1. 10–20 seconds, one task each. Longer is a course; shorter is a Coachmark.
  2. A real expert in uniform, in a real home, portrait, phone-quality light. Never stock, never a model.
  3. Voice is a recorded native speaker describing what is on screen, present tense — "Tap Check in when you reach the door." Never "In this video…".
  4. Captions always on, burned in per language, ≤ 2 lines of ≤ 40 chars, changing no faster than every 2 s.
  5. Loops once, then Replay. No scrubber, speed, share, landscape, or next-clip autoplay.
  6. Offline: onboarding, go-live and check-in clips ship in the app (≤ 2 MB). Others download on Wi-Fi, or on tap with a Toast Using data · 1.5 MB — the one place a size is shown, because it costs her money.
  7. Watched at 80 % once; it can satisfy a Stepper step.
