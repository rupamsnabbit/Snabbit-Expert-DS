Core component

Chip

A small tappable choice in a row of choices. The one small control allowed to be a pill, because a row of pills says "pick from these" the way nothing else does. A Tag is read; a Chip is tapped; a Button does.

ProposedTier 1 · CoreSnabbitChip40 dp · 48 dp row≤ 6 per row
Three jobs

Kinds

Filter picks exactly one and the list below changes now. All is first and selected by default. When the list is about something she already has, such as her hours a day, her current value is selected instead, the chips run in natural order and All comes last (added 2026-10-01 for timings lists). Choice toggles zero or more and the screen's Button commits. Quick reply fires once and the row disappears. Answer (added 2026-10-01 for the attendance flow) has two chosen states and no unselected one: a shift starts as Coming (check) and a tap flips it to Can't come (moon). Removable (added 2026-10-07 for the work-area picker, projects/hood-shift-change) shows a value she already picked, in the selected look with no check, and a trailing ✕ that takes it back.

  • Selected changes shape: the leading icon becomes a check, the fill goes brand-subtle, the border goes brand. Never border colour alone (P1).
  • ≤ 6 chips. More categories than that is a Select sheet — a row must be scannable in one look.
  • The Camera / Gallery "Selection Pill" in Figma is a SelectionCard, not a chip.
Behaviour

States

Try it

Live: filter row

Tap a chip. The list filters within 300 ms; the row scrolls with a fade at the edge to say "more"; All never scrolls off.

Counts

A count on a filter chip tells her what's behind it before she taps. Integer only, ≤ 99 then 99+. Shown only when it helps her choose — time ranges (Today · This week) don't need one.

Try it

Live: quick replies

Complete, sendable sentences in the expert's voice. Tap one — it sends and the row goes away. Trailing paper-plane-right says these do something.

Try it

Live: picked areas

Removable chips over a capped multi-select list (up to 3). Each row keeps its map-pin leading and has its checkbox trailing; a picked row is tinted. Tap a row to pick it; tap a chip's ✕ to take it back, and its row unticks. At 3 of 3 the other rows go quiet and lose their box.

The ✕ is the only target

The chip has the selected look with no check, because the list already shows the checks. The ✕ hit area is 48 × 48, centred on the icon, and may run past the chip's edge (outlined below). The label does nothing, so a mis-tap never removes a pick. The strip wraps; it never scrolls.

  • One state, shown twice: removing a chip unticks its row; ticking a row adds its chip at the end of the strip.
  • Row titles are SemiBold here because this is a choice list (list-item.md § Content); the weight never changes on selection.
  • At the cap, unpicked rows use textDisabled, no checkbox, and keep the empty slot so the column stays straight. The counter 3 of 3 is the reason in words.
