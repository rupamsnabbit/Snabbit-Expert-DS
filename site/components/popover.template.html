Popover

A small surface anchored to the thing she just tapped, holding two or three actions about that thing, with the rest of the screen still visible and live. Tap the customer's house on the arrival map: Directions · Call Radhika. Tap outside: gone. It exists for the one case a BottomSheet handles badly — actions about a spot on the screen that the sheet would hide. No scrim, no three-dots menus, no lists of choices.

Kinds

actions: two or three 48 dp rows, icon 20 iconBrand + BodyLg verb, danger last in textError and always confirmed in a sheet. detail: a name, one fact, one tertiary Button. Surface bgPrimary, radius.lg, 1 dp borderDefault, elevation.floating — the border, not the shadow, separates it in sunlight. Arrow above or below only.

Small by rule

Rules

  1. Opens on tap of an anchor drawn as tappable — a marker on the taller map. Never on long-press (Tooltip), never by itself, never on scroll.
  2. Non-modal. No scrim; the screen stays live. Closes on tap outside, back, a scroll over 8 dp, a row, or a screen-level change. Never on a timer.
  3. Two or three rows. One row is a tap; four is a BottomSheet.
  4. Closes, then acts, so the outcome is never behind it. Leaving the app shows arrow-square-out.
  5. Danger last, in textError, and confirmed in a sheet — a row never completes a costly action alone.
  6. Same shape offline. Rows that need the network are disabled with the reason, never hidden.

What changes in Figma

  1. Create Popover on the Components page: Kind (Actions / Detail), Arrow (Above / Below), Rows (2 / 3), Danger last (boolean). Surface bgPrimary, radius.lg, borderDefault, effect = the IconButton floating shadow style.
  2. Row = a ListItem instance at 48 dp (icon 20 + BodyLg, no trailing), so a change to ListItem flows here.
  3. Place one instance on the arrival-map mock over the house marker (Directions · Call Radhika) with the marker selected.
  4. Benchmark P2 Popover → closed against this spec.

Result: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 16 variants, from nothing. No token proposals — elevation.floating plus the border; if the shadow is too light over a map the answer is the border, not a new level.
