Core component

Stepper

A short list of things to do, in order, with one of them lit. Onboarding, going live, a first check-in. It makes a multi-day process feel like a short list — and points at exactly one next step.

ProposedTier 1 · CoreSnabbitStepperrail · listOne Button, on the current step
Two layouts

Rail and list

The rail says where am I: 2–5 nodes with icon and label, non-interactive, at the top of a flow. The list says what do I do: one row per step inside a Card; in ordered mode only the current row carries a Button; in unordered mode (independent steps, as on the shipped Training screen) every open row is tappable and the flow owns the one Button. More than five steps is always a list.

Five states

States

Done is a green check and the word Done. Current is a brand ring, a brand wash and the Button. Upcoming and locked are grey with a reason — a grey thing with no reason is a broken thing (P5); the lock says her own action won't open it yet. Attention is a step that came back (ID photo rejected): amber ring, and it takes over as the one action.

Try it

Live

Complete the current step: the node turns to a check (300 ms), the connector fills, the next row lights and its Button appears; success sound and a medium haptic once. Tap an upcoming row to see the Toast.

Rules

Steps complete in order; the Stepper is told each state, it never infers. The rail is never tappable — wanting to jump to a step is the list. Spoken on entry: "Step 2 of 4, ID proof".
