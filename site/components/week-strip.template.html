WeekStrip

Seven days, Monday first, read as a record: which days counted. Not the DatePicker strip — that is for choosing a day; this is for seeing a week she has already lived.

ProposedIn app 2026-09-22Tier 1 · CoreSnabbitWeekStrip2 kinds · markersRead-only
Two messages

band · dots

A band says these days ran together — one unbroken pill. Dots say each day did or did not count. A band that breaks is two pills with a gap, and the gap is the most important pixel on the card.
