The shipped discs are blue, green and pink

On the Coins screen the three earn discs are each a different hue. That is colour meaning only different, which P6 reserves for status. The spec says one disc colour with identity carried by the icon — and the shipped fills stay until the design lead decides, because reusing them costs tech nothing and changing them costs a sprint. That order — record, reuse, then decide — is the rule this component was written under.
