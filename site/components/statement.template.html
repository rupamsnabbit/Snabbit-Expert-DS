Schedule row status · 2026-10-29

Where the money is, on the row

Five tones, one meaning each: blue it is moving, amber we are fixing it, green done, red she must act. The date tile shows while the money is coming; once something has happened the same slot holds a flat status disc and the line carries the only date — two dates on one row would make her guess which is hers. The meaning of each payout state is patterns/payout.md § 2.
