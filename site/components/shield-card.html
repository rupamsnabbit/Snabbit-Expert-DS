Composition

ShieldCard (Kavach)

The in-job safety feature — audio recorded, location shared, the team checks on her — composed from Card, Button, SafetyStrip, BottomSheet and VoicePrompt into one row on the job card that says, plainly and continuously, whether Shield is on. Around it: a consent sheet (once), the first-start explainer, and the safety check.

Last row of the in-job Card

ShieldRow · states

Six states, each said in words and shown by a second channel — a pulsing record dot and a live level meter when on, a warning glyph and the one fix when blocked. One Button per state at most. The blue Kavach shield is the only domain-coloured pictogram in the app (to confirm).

Try it

Row · live

Check in and watch Shield start by itself (the proposed default). Pull the battery down, run out of storage, or tap the row to open the Shield sheet with its costly-switch-off confirmation.

before check-in · consent given · Shield will start at check-in
Around the row

Consent · SafetyCheck

Consent once, before the first check-in, with a visible way to say no. The safety check is Shield at work: two plain-tap answers, spoken and repeated, no answer in 30 s treated as danger. Help is on the way is SOSControl's active screen, not a second one.

Behaviour and content

Rules

  1. Starts at check-in after consent; the row reports it. A per-account Switch in Profile turns it off with a confirmation — a costly switch-off. Product decision to confirm.
  2. Never blocks work. Blocked reasons are checked before check-in so the fix happens at the door; check-in is allowed either way.
  3. No modal during a job. Blocked and stopped live in the row with a spoken line and a Snackbar; the SafetyStrip carries the state when she leaves the screen.
  4. Status says two facts: Recording · Location shared. Never Monitoring active.
  5. Reasons say the fix: Not enough storage → Free up space.
  6. Stop confirms; I am safe does not. Stopping protection is costly; ending a false alarm must be easy.
  7. Kavach blue on the shield mark only. Every control is a system Button. Decision to confirm.
  8. Buttons: Start Shield · Start again · Stop Shield · Keep it on · Yes, turn on Shield · Not now · I am in danger · I am safe. Never Activate, Start Monitoring, I will do, Yes, I agree.

  3. Are you in danger? → SafetyCheck: BottomSheet alert; emoji removed; Help is on the way frames → SOSControl / Active screen instances.

  5. Add the shield kind to SafetyStrip; rebuild the activation frames on the current in-job header.
