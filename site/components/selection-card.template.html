Core component

SelectionCard

A Card that behaves like a Radio or a Checkbox. For options that need more than a label — a description, a price, an illustration, two facts. The card is the tap target; the control inside shows the state.

ProposedTier 1 · CoreSnabbitSelectionCardcompact · rich · date · heroReplaces 8 frames
Four shapes

Variants

Control always leading, top-left — the same place as a Radio row. Compact for one-line options with an icon; rich for options she compares, with facts and a 64 dp asset; date for picking a day, in a strip of ≤ 7; hero when the picture is the option — image 16:9 on top, radio or checkbox under it, ≤ 3 per group.

Behaviour

States

Selected is border + control + a check badge on the asset — three channels. The fill stays white; a pink wash across several multi-selected cards is loud. At most one Recommended per group.
