Core component

SelectionCard

A Card that behaves like a Radio or a Checkbox. For options that need more than a label — a description, a price, an illustration, two facts. The card is the tap target; the control inside shows the state.

Four shapes

Variants

Control always leading, top-left — the same place as a Radio row. Compact for one-line options with an icon; rich for options she compares, with facts and a 64 dp asset (its shift option form, the shipped timings card, puts pay where the asset was); date for picking a day, in a strip of ≤ 7; hero when the picture is the option — image on top (4:3, or 16:9 for photos), radio or checkbox under it, ≤ 3 per group.

In app · 2026-10-01

Shift option

This is the shipped timings card as a rich form, used on every timings list. The landmark sits over sector · km; the area is the list heading. A text button Show map (map-pin 16 + brand text, no container) ends the first rail line and opens the hood as a MapSnippet in a bottom sheet; it never selects the card. Pay is in a trailing slot that never shrinks: Earn up to · ₹ · a month, in neutral text, because green means money already credited. Below a divider, the day is a rail. A split day is block · break · block joined by a dashed connector, so she sees where the gap falls without a type word. Tap a card to select it.

  • Pay is neutral, not green. Green means money already credited (P6, Amount positive).
  • Earn up to … a month, not Earn upto. Upto is a misspelling, and the period stops her guessing.
  • 8 am – 2 pm · 6 hrs, not 8 AM to 2 PM | 6 hrs. This follows content.md § 5.
  • The hours add up and the break shows its length. Blocks add up to the day's hours, and the break equals the gap between them (P9, P1).
  • The text column shrinks and wraps first: hood up to 2 lines, area line up to 2 lines, never truncated. The pay slot keeps its width.
Behaviour

States

Selected is border + control + a check badge on the asset — three channels. The fill stays white; a pink wash across several multi-selected cards is loud. At most one Recommended per group.

What changes in Figma

  1. Merge the six selection frames into SelectionCard: Variant (Compact / Rich / Date), Mode (Single / Multi), State, booleans Description, Facts, Asset, Recommended.
  2. Control leading top-left; delete the bottom-divider layout.
  3. Selected = borderBrand 2 + control + check badge on the asset.
  4. Facts to textBody with Phosphor icons; amounts as Amount.
  5. Selection Pill → Compact; Service and Shift cards → Rich instances on feature pages.
  6. Rich gains the shift option form, taken from the shipped card: Pay (label · Amount md · period) in place of Asset, a Divider, and Day (Single / Split, with clock and coffee instances).

Result: ~30 variants replacing 8 frames.
