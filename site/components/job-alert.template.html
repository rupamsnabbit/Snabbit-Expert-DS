Composition · the reference case

JobAlert

A job arrives, she has about ninety seconds, and the money is on the line. One full-screen takeover built from the system's own parts: facts in a glance, the earnings as a number she can trust, the time as a shape, one Button under the thumb, and a decline that is safe but protected. The flow around it — sound, wake, overlay, the three exits — is the new-job alert pattern.

Behaviour and content

Rules

  1. Sound first. sound.newJob loops until she acts or the window closes; it overrides silent (alarm channel), follows alarm volume.
  2. One Button. Accept job is the only primary; Decline is a ghost danger Button above it with a 600 ms hold. Never a third Button, never a confirmation sheet.
  3. Time is the Timer's. Timer L above the Button, tone computed; no time-fill inside the Button, no red wash.
  4. Four facts, then the money. Distance · start · duration in a strip; You'll earn + Amount L + the breakdown expanded, ≤ 5 rows, one conditional row.
  5. Penalty before it applies. A repeat alert shows the Nudge loss with the red-card asset and the amount.
  6. Honest offline. Banner + Accept disabled with the reason under it; the Timer keeps running; retry every 3 s.
  7. Safety wins. The alert waits behind SOS and SafetyCheck; Help and SOS stay live in the header.
  8. Copy: New job · You'll earn · Accept within · Accept job · Decline → Declined · Time's up · This job went to someone else. Never Deny, ACCEPT IN, You Will Earn.

  3. Long Distance out of the breakdown into the facts strip; 🌞 → reward asset.
  4. Red wash + ACCEPT IN → Timer L urgent tone.
  5. Point Over the app and the lock-screen notification at the same instance.
