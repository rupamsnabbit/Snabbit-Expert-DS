Core component

DatePicker

Choosing a day the way she thinks about days: this week as a row of big numbers, further away as a month she can see whole. Never three spinning wheels, never a typed 10/06/2026. What she chose is written in words before she confirms.

ProposedTier 1 · CoreSnabbitDatePickerstrip · monthTime is a Select, not a picker
This week

Strip

Two to seven days from today as 64 dp tiles — big number, weekday under it, Today on the first. Single select. It never scrolls; more than seven days is a month. Tap a tile.

Log

tap a day
Further away

Month

Seven columns, Monday first, 44 dp cells with 4 dp gutters. Today is ringed; selected is filled; a range is a soft band between two filled ends. The summary line under the grid is the confirmation — the Button belongs to the flow and stays disabled until the summary has a value. Try a range of up to 6 days.

Controls

range · up to 6 days
One cell

Day cell states

One DayCell in both kinds. No red Sundays — red means error or SOS; a Sunday is not a mistake and many experts work Sundays. A weekly off day says Off. The amount slot exists only in the read-only earnings view.

Never a wheel

Time

Times are a select BottomSheet of named slots, or a Chip row when there are four or fewer. Wheels of numbers are the worst control for this user; slots are how shifts actually work.

What changes in Figma

  1. Calendar (×2) + Date Picker + Date + Day Properties + Day Strip → DatePicker: Kind (Strip / Month), Select (Single / Range), Amounts, YearStepper; DayCell: State (Default / Today / Selected / InRange / Disabled / Off), Amount.
  2. Remove the red Sunday colour; add the Off caption.
  3. Delete DOB Selection (TextField) — replace with DatePicker month + YearStepper.
  4. Date Selection Cards → strip tile instances.
  5. The summary line becomes part of the component; the separate Confirm Leave sheet goes.

Result: ~14 variants replacing 7 frames and 20-odd loose cells. caret-left / caret-right join the IconButton allowed set for the month header only.
