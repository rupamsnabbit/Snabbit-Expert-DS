Core component

Tabs · Segmented

Two ways to switch what a screen shows without leaving it. Segmented for two or three views of the same thing; Tabs for three to five sections of one screen. Both change content in place, remember the choice, and never navigate — the tab bar at the bottom is the map; these are pages of one book.

Views of the same thing

Segmented

A bgTertiary track with a raised bgPrimary segment — readable in greyscale and outdoors, unlike the shipped dark fill. Two or three equal segments, full width or hugging inside a card header. Labels only, ≤ 10 chars; an optional count.

Sections of one screen

Tabs

Three to five equal-width tabs with a 2 dp brand underline that slides. Never scrolls — a cut-off tab is a hidden feature and a mis-tap; six becomes a select sheet. Icon with label or label alone, never icon-only. One marker Tag above at most one tab; badges on at most two.

Choosing

Which one

  1. Same data, different slice (today / week / month; weekdays / weekends) → Segmented, 2–3.
  2. Different sections of one screen (shift lengths; earnings / bonus / penalties) → Tabs, 3–5.
  3. Two lists that are both worth seeing (upcoming / past leave) → headings, not tabs. Two lists shown beat two lists hidden.
  4. More than five → a select BottomSheet or a Chip row.
  5. Anything that goes somewhere or does something → a Button or ListItem, never a tab.

What changes in Figma

  1. Create Segmented: Count (2 / 3), Size (M / S), Selected, Counts. Track bgTertiary; segment bgPrimary + borderDefault.
  2. Create Tabs: Count (3–5), Selected, Icons, Marker, per-tab Badge. Indicator bgBrand.
  3. Rebuild My Rate Card: Segmented M for Weekdays / Weekends; Tabs (5) for shift lengths with the Your shift marker; no horizontal scroll.
  4. Leave history stays as headings.
