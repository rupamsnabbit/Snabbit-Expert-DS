Core component

Accordion

A row that opens to say more — and reads it aloud. Two jobs: answer a question, or show a breakdown of an amount. It hides information, never actions.

ProposedTier 1 · CoreSnabbitAccordionfaq · breakdownSpeaker on every row
Two jobs

Kinds

faq: the question she would ask, a speaker that reads the answer, a caret. breakdown: title and Amount M collapsed; open, the Amount L above its line rows, one of which may be conditional — bgInfo wash, clock, the condition — meaning you get this if…

Limits

Rules

  1. No actions inside. One tertiary link at most (Ask Saathi ›), never a Button. If it needs a tap, it goes on the screen (P2).
  2. Never nested; never more than 6 items in a group — beyond that it is a search or a Select.
  3. Whole header toggles; the speaker is its own 48 dp target and does not toggle.
  4. A breakdown that is the only thing on the screen opens by default; in a list it starts collapsed.
  5. FAQ titles are questions she would ask, ≤ 48 chars. Bodies end with what to do.
  6. Phosphor only in row labels — the Summer Bonus 🌞 emoji becomes sun.
