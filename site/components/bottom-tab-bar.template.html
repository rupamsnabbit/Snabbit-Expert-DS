Core component

BottomTabBar

The whole map of the app, always visible, always in the same order. For a user who struggles with hierarchical navigation this is the only structure she needs to learn. Everything is reachable from one of these tabs in at most two steps.

ProposedTier 1 · CoreSnabbitBottomTabBar5 tabs · fixed orderNew — no Figma component existed
Fixed

Destinations

Five, in this order, in every build. New areas go inside a tab, never as a sixth. Saathi and Rewards move down here from the home header, where they had no labels.

Behaviour

States

Selected is the icon's weight (regular → fill) plus two colours — no underline, no pill. Labels are always visible; an icon-only tab bar is not allowed. At most two badged tabs at once; Home and Profile never badge.

Try it

Live

Switch tabs — instant, no slide; each tab keeps its scroll. Badges clear when the tab opens. Tapping the current tab scrolls it to the top.

Rules

Always visible on tab roots and one level down. Hidden only on full-screen flows that must not be left mid-way: job alert, check-in camera, OTP. Never hides on scroll.

Labels wrap to two lines at 130 % font scale and the bar grows to 72 dp — they never truncate or hide.

What changes in Figma

  1. Create BottomTabBar: Tabs (4 / 5), Selected (1–5), per-tab Badge (None / Dot / Count).
  2. Phosphor instances: regular unselected, fill selected.
  3. Add it to every tab-root and one-level-down screen; remove hamburger menus, drawer frames and the Saathi / Help pill from the home header.
  4. Move Saathi and Rewards entry points into the bar.

Result: ~10 variants, and a bottom edge every screen agrees on.
