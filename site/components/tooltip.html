Tooltip

A small bubble with an arrow that says one short thing about the exact spot it points at, then leaves. label names an icon-only control on long-press; status announces a change to the thing it is pinned to — the Timer gained fifteen minutes — in that thing's own status colour, for three seconds. No scrim, no voice lesson, no choices: those are the Coachmark, the Popover and the BottomSheet.

Kinds

label on bgInverse, BodyMd Medium, ≤ 24 characters, arrow above or below sliding to the anchor. status on bgSuccess / bgWarning / bgError with a 1 dp tone border, a 20 dp tone icon and text*Strong (the 700 step, proposed for 0.20 — shown here). The tone is computed from the Timer it points at, never chosen.

Small by rule

Rules

  1. Never on tap. A tap does something; a Tooltip is never the result of one. There is no i in this app.
  2. Never a lesson, never a choice. No button, speaker, counter or link inside. A lesson is a Coachmark; two actions are a Popover.
  3. Arrow above or below. The arrow slides to the anchor's centre; the bubble stays 16 dp from the edges and never covers the anchor, SOS or the primary Button.
  4. status colour is computed from the Timer's tone or the outcome — calm → success, attention → warning, urgent → error, sent → success, failed → error.
  5. One at a time. A new one replaces the old; status leaves after timeout.toast; label leaves on release.
  6. Words. label ≤ 24 chars, the screen-reader name; status ≤ 40 chars, number first, spoken once.
