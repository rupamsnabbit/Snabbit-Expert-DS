PromoCard

A card that offers her something — a referral bonus, a contest prize, a festival bonus — with a picture of it on the pink ground, one line saying what it is, and one Button that starts it. Twenty-two loose frames in six colours become one shape.

Structure

Anatomy

Container as any Card: bgPrimary, borderDefault, radius.lg, flat. A bgBrandSubtle media ground 120 dp tall holds an optional eyebrow Tag and a 96 dp asset. Title BodyLg Medium centred, two lines max; optional one-line body; one Button M full width. The card itself is not tappable — the Button is the one target.

Common mistakes

Don't

✦ Rewards Jeeto 🏆 ✦
Saheli jodo, kapdon ki tension chhodo!
Don't
A tinted card, a coloured border, an orange button, an emoji eyebrow, a noun with an exclamation mark as the Button. Colour by prize is decorative colour (P6); the Button is a verb (content.md § 3).
KAMAO 15,000
Saheli lao, saath mein ₹15,000 tak kamao
Don't
An amount painted into the artwork and a 19-character label. Words in an asset cannot be translated or read aloud (assets.md); the amount is in the title; the Button is Invite on WhatsApp.
