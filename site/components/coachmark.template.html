Core component

Coachmark

Point and say. The first time a screen shows her something new, a Coachmark dims everything else, lights the one thing, and speaks one sentence about it in her language. Then it never comes back for that thing. It is the app's only tooltip.

Parts

Anatomy

Scrim at 60 % with a spotlight cut around the target (+ 8 dp, pulsing ring twice). The target stays live. A bubble on bgPrimary, 296 dp wide, 12 dp from the target, arrow pointing at its centre: an optional pictogram, one BodyLg sentence, a speaker that replays the voice, and one way out — Got it, or the target itself.

Try it

Live tour

First launch after onboarding: three stops — availability, the tab bar, SOS. The bubble sits below a target in the top half and above one in the bottom half. Tapping the scrim does not dismiss (the bubble shakes instead); the availability stop is do-it — flipping the switch is the way out.

Controls

Tour shows once per account. "Show me again" lives in Saathi → Help.
When

Rules

  1. Once. Per control, per account, on any device. Replay from Saathi → Help.
  2. One at a time. One Coachmark or tour per screen visit; the next feature waits for the next visit.
  3. Never during a job alert, a running Timer, check-in or OTP; never while an error or offline Banner shows; never on the very first screen of the first launch.
  4. Never over SOS. SOS is never under a scrim — it is either the target or it stays lit and live outside.
  5. The scrim does not dismiss. A slip of the thumb must not lose the guidance.
  6. Voice plays automatically 300 ms after the bubble settles; the speaker replays. With sound off, the speaker shows speaker-slash and the one sentence carries the meaning.
  7. Three stops is the ceiling. Four sentences means a redesign or a how-to clip.

What changes in Figma

  1. Tooltips → Coachmark: Placement (Below / Above), Media (None / Picto), WayOut (Do-it / Got-it), Counter (None / n of 3), Sound (On / Muted). Bubble bgPrimary, text BodyLg.
  2. Delete Colored Tooltip. Delete the four side-arrow variants.
  3. Add a Coachmark / Overlay frame — scrim + spotlight mask — to compose over screen mocks.
  4. Any mock that annotates a value with a tooltip ("This includes all bonuses") → info BottomSheet or speaker-high beside the label.

Result: ~10 variants replacing 8, and a first-run pattern every screen mock can show. Token proposal: overlay.scrim = bgInverse @ 60 %, shared with BottomSheet.
