Core component · P4

VoicePrompt

The one visible shape for "this can be heard". A bar that speaks a whole screen, an inline control that speaks one thing. The shipped onboarding already has the bar; this names it, adds the second form, and writes the rules for when the app speaks on its own.

Shape

Two forms

The bar sits under the header: a play/pause disc, a progress track with no knob (progress is information, not a control — dragging 12 dp one-handed on a bus fails P3), and a sound on/off toggle for autoplay. The inline control is an IconButton M tertiary at the trailing edge of the thing it reads, with a Listen label when there is room. Playing: pause icon, filled track, filled inline disc.

Try it

Live

Press play on the bar, then Listen on the address: starting one stops the other. Toggle sound off and the bar stops autoplaying (a Toast confirms); tap play still works. Finished → the disc returns to play and replays from the start.

Policy

What gets a voice

Anything that autoplays also shows its control, so what spoke can be replayed and stopped. In-job screens never speak unasked — a customer's home is not the place for a phone to start talking.

Behaviour

Rules

  1. One voice at a time. Starting one stops any other on the screen.
  2. Priority. Job-alert sound and SOS voice stop and block everything else; a phone call pauses voice and it does not resume by itself.
  3. Language. Her chosen language. Fixed copy is a recorded native speaker; names, amounts and times are on-device TTS spliced in. Safety copy is never synthetic.
  4. One speed. Replay is the control — no 1.5×, no slow mode.
  5. Offline. Fixed-copy audio ships in the app in four languages; voice never needs the network.
  6. Autoplay is once. A screen that has spoken does not speak again on return. With TalkBack on, autoplay is suppressed; the controls stay.
  7. Written as speech. ≤ 12 words a sentence, ≤ 3 sentences a screen; what a trainer would say standing beside her. Reviewed aloud by a native speaker before ship (P9).

What changes in Figma

  1. Create VoicePrompt: Form (Bar / Inline), State (Idle / Playing / Finished), Sound (On / Off), Label (inline). Bar bound to bgBrandSubtle, disc to bgBrand.
  2. Replace the scrubber knob in every onboarding frame with the knob-less track; add the bar to training, go-live and error screens.
  3. Replace the Listen pill and bare speakers with inline instances.
  4. Note autoplay in each screen mock's description: autoplays first visit / never autoplays.

Result: ~8 variants, and the app's voice drawn on every screen that has one. Token proposals: voice.autoplayDelay 500 ms; sound.voiceStart (tick haptic only).
