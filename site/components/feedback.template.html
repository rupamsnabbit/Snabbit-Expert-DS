Core components

Banner · Toast · Snackbar

Three ways the app talks back, chosen by how long the thing is true. Still true in ten seconds — Banner. Needs an action — Snackbar. Everything else — Toast.

ProposedTier 1 · CoreInline soft = conditionFloating dark = eventOffline is a Banner
Choosing

Which one

Banner

A condition that lasts

Inline, in the layout, pushes content down. Soft tone. Never auto-dismisses. Offline, verification pending, bonus unlocked this week, complete by 15 March.

Toast

An event that just happened

Floats at the bottom for 3 s, then gone. Solid dark with a coloured icon. No action, never the only place the result appears.

Snackbar

An event she can act on

Toast plus exactly one action — Undo, Retry, View. Holds 6 s. The second chance that lets declining a job stay one tap.

Lasting

Banner

Always soft. Leading Phosphor icon in the tone colour — or one asset pictogram, which is exactly what the asset layer is for. One action or one dismiss, never both; the Offline banner has neither.

Passing

Toast & Snackbar

Always solid bgInverse — a floating message must read over any content in sunlight. The icon is mandatory: it is the only thing that tells success from error in greyscale.

Try it

Live

Fire toasts to see the queue (one at a time). Decline a job to get an Undo snackbar. Toggle offline to see the Banner take the page slot and the Back online toast when it clears.

Reconciliation

What changes in Figma

  1. Merge the four banner frames into Banner: Tone × Variant (Standard / Facts / Offline / Degraded) with Body, Action, Dismiss, Leading (Icon / Asset).
  2. Toast: one solid bgInverse style with a Tone icon; delete soft and status-coloured solids. Move No internet / Bad internet to Banner Offline / Degraded; storage becomes an Error Banner with Clear space.
  3. Add Snackbar: Toast + one action slot.
  4. Replace emoji with Phosphor or asset instances; rebind colours.

Result: Banner ~16 · Toast 5 · Snackbar 5, replacing 21 mixed variants.
