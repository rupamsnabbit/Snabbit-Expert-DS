Core component

Amount

The primary number on most screens — what a job pays, what today earned, what reached her bank. One component so the ₹, grouping, size, colour and sign are always the same.

ProposedTier 1 · CoreSnabbitAmount4 sizes × 6 tones × 6 statesIndian grouping · tabular
Sizing

Sizes

The ₹ sits one type step below the digits and aligns to their cap height, so the number reads first. One hero per screen.

Meaning

Tones & signs

Sign is a property of the value, not typed into copy. Positive and negative differ by colour and sign and an arrow icon when the number stands alone — never colour alone. charge is money she spends, not money she loses: it looks like default and must always carry a caption saying where it goes.

Behaviour

States

A loading amount is never ₹0 — zero is a real, alarming number to someone paid by the job. A locked amount says how to unlock it.

Rules

Formatting

Type any number. Indian grouping, no paise unless non-zero, Latin numerals in every language.

  • ₹ always — never Rs, INR or /-.
  • Groups: 1,250 · 12,500 · 1,25,000 · 12,50,000.
  • Ranges ₹400–450; rates ₹169/hr with the unit in textSecondary.
  • Read aloud as words — "four hundred and fifty rupees" — never digit by digit.

Reconciliation

What changes in Figma

  1. Rename Value → Amount. Properties: Size (Hero / Lg / Md / Sm), Tone (Default / Positive / Negative / Brand / Muted), State (Default / Locked / Loading / Hidden / Estimated), booleans Label, Caption, Trailing chevron.
  2. ₹ as its own text layer one step smaller, cap-aligned.
  3. Replace every hand-typed ₹ in Earnings, Payout and Job card frames with an Amount instance.
  4. Bind digits to the type scale and colours to semantic variables.
