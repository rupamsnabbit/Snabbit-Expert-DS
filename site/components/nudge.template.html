Core component · P6

Nudge

One line that ties what she does next to what it is worth — a coin if she logs in by 6:40, a red card if she lets the job lapse. Always attached to the control it is about, never blocking it, gone the moment the condition is settled.

gain on the proposed bgReward tint — the coin's own yellow, not a warning — with an arrow and the coin; loss on bgError with a clock or warning and the red card or a minus. Rare by rule

Rules

  1. Attached. 8 dp under the Button or card it is about, same width. Never in a header, a sheet's action area, or as a Banner.
  2. One per screen. A list counts as one. Two nudges is a rulebook.
  3. Only while open. Appears while the deadline has not passed or the choice is not made; collapses when settled — good outcomes hand off to the reward moment, bad ones to a Banner.
  4. Never repeats. A recurring condition (daily login) is new each day; a dismissed or acted-on Nudge does not return the same day.
  5. Silent. No sound, no haptic, not spoken on its own — the Button or Timer beside it already has the voice.
  6. Words. Condition then consequence, ≤ 40 chars; asset + count carry the amount; times as 6:40 am; never Hurry!
