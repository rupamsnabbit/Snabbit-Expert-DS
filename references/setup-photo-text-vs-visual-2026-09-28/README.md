## The same task, twice

The task: before the job starts, lay out the products and send one photo of the setup.

| | `01` — told | `02` — shown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Screens | One long scroll | Four moments: arrange → camera → saving → started |
| How the task is carried | Heading *Set up, then send a photo* + two numbered text steps + a sub-caption rule (*Only your things in the photo — not Radhika*) | A scene of a good setup; one line on its band |
| Time | Not shown | Timer ring, *04:23* |
| On screen besides the task | Customer card (name, timing, duration), Kavach banner, nine product rows in two Accordions | Nothing — Help and SOS in the header only |
| Illustration | Placeholder, with the text written to cover for it | The picture is the instruction |
| Success | — | Tick, *Job started*, a scene of greeting at the door, *Say Namaste* |

## What each difference breaks, as one sentence

- `01` makes text carry the instruction that the scene in `02` carries (P1 · *Text is the last medium*).
- `01` lists the steps as numbered lines she must read and hold; `02` makes each step its own screen (P1 · *A step is a screen*).
- `01` puts a rule in a caption; `02` has no caption, because a picture framed on the kit would carry the rule (P1 · *An instruction a picture can show is a picture*).
- `01` repeats the customer card and a Kavach prompt that this step does not need (P1 · *Related is not needed*, P2).
- `01`'s text was written to stand in for a placeholder illustration and would have outlived it (P1 · *A missing picture is not a reason for text*).
- `01` shows no time; `02` shows the window as a draining ring (P5).

## What `02` still owes the system

These are copy and detail, not direction; recorded here, not fixed here.

- *OK* on *Job Started* is banned (`content.md` § 9); the Button names what happens next.
- *TIME LEFT* in caps is retired (`timer.md`, `content.md` § 2).
- *Complete Set up* opens the camera; the action is *Take photo*.
- The camera screen's helper line (*Please ensure all products required are here*) repeats what the scene already said.
- Whether she needs the product list at the arrange step at all is open — if she does, it is one quiet row behind a tap, not an open list.
